Artisanal Craftsmanship

The Alchemy of Olfaction

From raw botanical sourcing to hand-blending, we uphold centuries-old French perfumery traditions.

I

Ethical Sourcing

Direct partnerships with sustainable flower farms in Grasse, France and Mysore, India ensures pure, high-grade oils.

II

Slow Maceration

Every batch ages quietly in glass carboys for six weeks to allow complex notes to fuse together seamlessly.

III

Glassmorphism Vessels

Housed in custom frosted glass bottles designed to shield precious oils from light while displaying understated elegance.
